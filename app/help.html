Open Herd Records

Start on Home

Open Your herd. Tap a number to see those animals. Meat holds, milk holds and animals needing a check include sold and archived cattle.

Choose Add a calf, Add an animal, Record a treatment, Weigh, Move to a pasture or group, Breeding or pregnancy check, or Sold or died. Pick an animal, answer each question with Next, check your answers, then tap Save record. Back lets you change an answer. Extra details are available on the final review screen.

Today shows records to check and backup reminders. Type a tag or name to find an animal. Recent activity lets you review records. Treatment undo needs a reason and a hold review.

New here? Add your first animal, record something, then back up. Sample cattle live in a separate demo herd. Return to Your herd to leave or delete the demo. Demo imports, backups and exports are turned off to keep practice records separate.

Recorded interval elapsed never means safe for slaughter or milk. Follow the label and veterinarian instructions.

Prepare before leaving signal

Open the app online and wait for Ready offline. On iPhone or iPad Safari, open Share and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android Chrome, use Install app or Add to Home screen in the menu. On desktop Chrome or Edge, use the address-bar install control. On Mac Safari use Add to Dock where available. Firefox can use the app in an ordinary tab; install support varies.

Installation is optional. Browser and installed contexts may have separate records. Verify your herd and a saved backup in each context.

Make text bigger

Tap Backup. Find Text size. Pick Normal, Large or Extra large. This choice stays on this phone.

Record a treatment

Pick the animal, product, dose, how it was given, time, meat wait and milk wait. Then check and save. Same as last time can copy product and route answers. Last meat and milk answers have their own buttons. Read the shown product and wait before you tap. Check the bottle and your vet each time.

In the field

Open Animals, choose the dam and Add calf. Confirm the birth date and identity before saving. For treatment, enter the actual administration and supplied meat and milk instructions. Unknown stays Needs review. Wait for Saved on this phone before leaving a form.

Keep and restore records

Tap Save a copy on Home or Backup. Pick Files, email or another app. If sharing is not supported, the copy downloads instead. Keep it in Files. Pick Check my copy to check it against your herd. A cancelled share is not a saved copy. Restore starts with a preview; replacement also requires a saved, verified copy of the current records. Keep copies in two places. Airplane mode does not prevent local exports, restore or report preparation.

Try a practice restore each month

Open Backup and pick Practice a restore. Choose a saved copy. The app checks the file and builds a preview. It does not replace your herd. If the copy is old, save a new one.

iPhone and iPad Safari

Safari may clear records after 7 days of Safari use with no visit here. Save a copy first. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open that app and pick I have a backup. Your Safari records and Home Screen records are separate. Never clear Safari to fix an update.

Updates and drafts

Updates download only while connected. Update when ready waits until every Herd Records tab is free of unsaved changes and calf drafts. Save or explicitly discard drafts, and close older tabs that cannot coordinate. A file format change requires a freshly saved and verified backup. An interrupted download keeps the old shell. Your records stay here if the update fails.

Privacy and optional news

Ordinary page and asset requests go to the web host. Signing up happens on manleyfarms.com. This app never sends your email or herd records anywhere. See Privacy.

Storage and recovery

No cloud copy or automatic sync exists. Clearing site data, private browsing, eviction or losing a device can remove records. Request a saved copy. The app asks the phone to keep records after your first animal and each saved copy. A refusal is retried after a week. If recovery appears, export the existing records and pre-migration snapshot; use a compatible release. Never clear storage to fix an update.
