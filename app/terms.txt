#### 3. Withdrawal information and animal products

There is no drug database or automatic verification of withdrawal periods. You supply the product, dose, route, administration time, instructions and separate meat and milk withdrawal rules. Obtain those rules from the applicable label or your veterinarian's instructions for the actual use. Changes in use, dose, route, animal class or other conditions can matter. Do not infer a withdrawal period from a fictional example, another animal's record, or a blank field.

A countdown is arithmetic on recorded inputs and the device clock. Missing or incorrect entries, open courses, repeated doses, time changes, unresolved instructions and software defects can affect the result. Non-time conditions require review. Prohibited use is not made permissible by waiting for a date or acknowledging a warning. “Recorded interval elapsed” never means safe for slaughter or milk. A displayed elapsed interval does not establish residue absence, food safety, fitness for slaughter, permission to sell milk, or legal compliance. Verify the actual history and applicable instructions before making a decision.
